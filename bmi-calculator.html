Calculate your Body Mass Index (BMI) from your height and weight and see your category — Underweight, Healthy, Overweight, or Obese. Free BMI calculator, no signup.

Enter your height and weight to find your Body Mass Index (BMI) and its category.

BMI categories

Below 18.5: Underweight · 18.5 – 24.9: Healthy weight · 25 – 29.9: Overweight · 30 and above: Obese. BMI is a rough guide only — it does not account for muscle, age or body type. For personal health advice, speak to a doctor. Your numbers stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Enter your height and weight.
  2. Click Calculate to see your BMI and your category.

FAQs

What is a healthy BMI?
A BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is generally considered healthy for most adults.

Is BMI a medical diagnosis?
No. BMI is a general guide only. For health advice, please talk to a doctor.
