How counting works

Words are groups of letters or numbers separated by spaces. Sentences end with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark. Paragraphs are blocks of text separated by blank lines. Everything is counted in your browser — your text is never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your text in the box.
  2. Words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs are counted live as you type.

FAQs

Does it count spaces?
Both counts are shown — characters with spaces and characters without spaces.

Is there a word limit?
No. You can paste an essay, an assignment, or a long article.
