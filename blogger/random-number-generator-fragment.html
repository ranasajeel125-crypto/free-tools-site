Random Number Generator

Set a minimum and a maximum, then press the button to get a random whole number between them — useful for lucky draws, games, and making a quick decision.

How it works

Every number between your minimum and maximum (including both ends) has an equal chance of being picked. The number is made in your browser using JavaScript's random number function.
