Mortgage Calculator

Work out your monthly mortgage payment from the home price, down payment, interest rate and loan term. Free mortgage calculator — see your loan amount, total interest and total cost. No signup.

Enter the home price and your down payment in the same currency, then the annual interest rate and how many years the loan runs for.

How the payment is worked out

Your loan amount is the home price minus your down payment. The monthly payment is worked out with the standard fixed-rate formula, using the monthly interest rate (annual rate divided by 12) and the total number of monthly payments. A bigger down payment or a lower rate means a smaller monthly payment and less interest overall; a longer term means a smaller monthly payment but much more interest in total. Your numbers stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Enter the home price and the down payment you will pay.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and pick the loan term.
  3. Click Calculate Payment to see your monthly payment, total interest and total cost.

FAQs

Does this include taxes and insurance?
No — this covers principal and interest only. Property taxes, home insurance and any other fees are extra, so your real monthly cost will be higher.

What is a good down payment?
20% is the usual target: it avoids extra insurance on many loans and keeps the monthly payment lower. A smaller down payment is possible, but the loan — and the interest — will be bigger.

Should I choose a shorter or longer term?
A shorter term (15 years) means a higher monthly payment but far less interest overall. A longer term (30 years) means a lower monthly payment but much more interest paid in total.
