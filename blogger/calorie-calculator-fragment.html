Calorie Calculator

Calculate how many calories you need per day to maintain, lose or gain weight. Free calorie calculator using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula — no signup.

Enter your age, gender, height, weight and activity level to find your daily calorie needs.

How the numbers work

Your BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the calories your body burns at rest. Multiplying it by your activity level gives your daily maintenance calories. Eating about 500 calories less per day usually leads to losing roughly 0.5 kg per week; eating about 500 more usually leads to gaining weight. These are estimates only — for personal diet advice, speak to a doctor or dietitian. Your numbers stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Enter your age, gender, height and weight.
  2. Pick the activity level that matches your week.
  3. Click Calculate to see your BMR and your daily calories for maintaining, losing and gaining weight.

FAQs

What formula does this use?
The Mifflin-St Jeor equation, the most widely used formula for estimating BMR.

How many calories do I need to lose weight?
As a rough guide, about 500 calories below your maintenance number per day. Do not go far below it without medical advice.

Is this medical advice?
No. This calculator gives general estimates only. For a diet plan, please talk to a doctor or dietitian.
