Image Compressor

Compress images online for free — shrink JPG, PNG and WebP photos to a smaller file size without losing much quality. Perfect for websites, WhatsApp, email attachments and online forms. No signup, no upload: your images never leave your browser.

Large photos slow down websites and fail on forms with size limits. This tool compresses them right on your device, so your pictures stay private.

How to use

  1. Choose one or more images from your device.
  2. Pick a quality — 80% is a good balance; lower means a smaller file.
  3. Click Compress Images, then download each compressed image below.

FAQs

Are my images uploaded anywhere?
No. Compression happens entirely in your browser — your photos never leave your device.

Which format should I choose?
JPG is smallest for photos. WebP is even smaller and works in all modern browsers. PNG keeps transparency but makes bigger files.

How much smaller will my image get?
A typical phone photo (3–5 MB) at 80% quality and 1200 px wide becomes roughly 150–400 KB — about 90% smaller — with barely visible quality loss.

Is it free?
Yes, completely free, no signup needed.
