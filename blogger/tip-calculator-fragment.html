Tip Calculator

Work out the tip, the total bill and each person's share in one click. Free tip calculator — enter the bill, pick a tip percent, and split it between friends if you like. No signup.

Enter the bill amount before the tip, choose how much to tip, and how many people are splitting the bill. The calculator shows the tip, the total, and what each person pays.

How the tip is worked out

The tip is the bill times the tip percent. The total is the bill plus the tip. If the bill is split, the total is divided equally between the people — each person pays the same share of the bill and the same share of the tip. Your numbers stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Enter the bill amount before any tip.
  2. Enter the tip percent, or tap 10%, 15% or 20%.
  3. Enter how many people are splitting the bill — leave it at 1 if you are paying alone.
  4. Click Calculate Tip to see the tip, the total and each person's share.

FAQs

How much is a 15% tip on a $100 bill?
The tip is $15.00 and the total is $115.00 — split between 4 people, each pays $28.75.

What is a good tip percent?
15% is standard in many places, 20% for great service, and 10% where tipping is less common. This calculator lets you try any percent.

Should the tip be calculated before or after tax?
Usually on the bill before tax — enter the pre-tax bill amount here and the tip will be worked out on that.
