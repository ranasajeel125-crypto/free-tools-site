How to use

  1. Enter the amount and pick the two currencies.

FAQs

Where do the rates come from?
Rates are fetched live from a free public exchange-rate service (exchangerate-api.com) each time the page loads. If the rates cannot be loaded, the converter will tell you — it never guesses a rate.

Is the converted amount exact?
The rate shown is the mid-market reference rate. Banks and money-transfer services add their own fee or margin, so the amount you actually receive may be slightly less.

Is it free?
Yes, completely free, no signup needed.
