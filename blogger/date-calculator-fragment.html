How the count is worked out

The days between two dates are counted on the calendar, including leap years — so 2024, a leap year, counts 29 February. The breakdown into years, months and days is worked out on the calendar too: whole years first, then whole months, then the days left over. Adding or subtracting days moves forward or back by that many calendar days. Your dates stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. To count days, pick a start date and an end date, then click Count the Days.
  2. To find a future or past date, pick a date, enter how many days, choose Add or Subtract, then click Find the Date.
  3. The answer also shows which day of the week each date falls on.

FAQs

Does it count leap years?
Yes — 29 February is counted in leap years such as 2024 and 2028, so the day count is exact.

Is the start date itself counted?
No — the count is the number of days from the start date to the end date. From 1 January to 2 January is 1 day.

Can I count backwards?
Yes — if the end date is before the start date, the result is shown as a negative number of days, with the same calendar breakdown.
