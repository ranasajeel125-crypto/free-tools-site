Merge PDF

Merge multiple PDF files into one PDF online, free. Combine scanned documents, bills, certificates or pages into a single file in the order you choose. No signup, no upload: merging happens in your browser, so your documents stay private.

Job applications, visa forms and office work often need one PDF instead of many separate files. This tool joins them in seconds.

    How to use

    1. Choose two or more PDF files — they merge in the order listed.
    2. Use the up/down buttons to change the order if needed.
    3. Click Merge PDFs, then download your single combined file.

    FAQs

    Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?
    No. Merging happens entirely in your browser using the pdf-lib library — your documents never leave your device.

    How many PDFs can I merge?
    There is no fixed limit, but very large files (hundreds of pages) may be slow on older phones. For most documents, 2–20 files merge in seconds.

    Does it change my PDFs?
    No. Pages are copied as they are — text, images and layout stay the same, just joined into one file.

    Is it free?
    Yes, completely free, no signup needed.
