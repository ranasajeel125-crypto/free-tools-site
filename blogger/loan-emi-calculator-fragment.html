FAQs

What is EMI?
EMI is the fixed amount you pay every month towards your loan. It covers both the loan amount and the interest, and it stays the same for the whole tenure when the rate is fixed.

How is EMI calculated?
EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly installments.
