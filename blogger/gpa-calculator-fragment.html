GPA Calculator

Calculate your GPA on the 4.0 scale from your course grades and credit hours. Free GPA calculator for students — no signup.

Enter each course grade and its credit hours, then click Calculate. Leave unused rows blank.

How GPA is calculated

Each grade is worth points on a 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0). Your GPA is the total grade points divided by the total credit hours, so a 3-credit course counts more than a 1-credit course. Some schools use a different scale, so check your own school's grading policy. Your grades stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. For each course, pick the grade and enter its credit hours.
  2. Leave rows blank for courses you do not have.
  3. Click Calculate GPA to see your GPA and total credit hours.

FAQs

What is a good GPA?
On a 4.0 scale, 3.5 and above is usually considered very good, and 3.0 is a solid average. Standards differ by school and country.

Does this use the 4.0 scale?
Yes — A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, F = 0.

Can I add more than 6 courses?
This version covers 6 courses at a time. For more, calculate two groups separately and combine them by total grade points ÷ total credits.
