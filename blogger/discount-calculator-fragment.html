Discount Calculator

Work out the sale price, how much you save and the final price with tax. Free discount calculator — enter the original price and the discount percent, and see the saving in one click. No signup.

Enter the original price and the discount. If tax is added at the till, enter the tax rate too and the final price will include it.

How the discount is worked out

The amount you save is the original price times the discount percent. The sale price is the original price minus that saving. If a tax rate is entered, the tax is charged on the sale price — after the discount, not before — and added to give the final price you pay. Your numbers stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Enter the original price before any discount.
  2. Enter the discount percent — for example 20 for 20% off.
  3. Enter the tax percent if tax applies, or leave it at 0.
  4. Click Calculate Discount to see how much you save and the final price.

FAQs

What is 20% off Rs 1,000?
You save Rs 200 and pay Rs 800 (before tax).

Is tax charged on the price before or after the discount?
After the discount — on the sale price. That is how shops normally charge it, and this calculator does the same.

Can I use it for a sale price I already know?
Yes — work backwards: if you know the price you paid and the discount, divide the price you paid by (1 − discount ÷ 100) to get the original price.
