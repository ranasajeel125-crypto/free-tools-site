Case Converter

Paste your text below, pick a case style, and copy the result. Handy for fixing headings, shouty text, or text that arrived in the wrong case.

What each style does

UPPER CASE makes every letter capital. lower case makes every letter small. Title Case capitalises the first letter of every word. Your text is changed in your browser only — nothing is uploaded.
